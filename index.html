Useful before the quote

Four things that change the result.

Start your project

01 Shade and film construction are separate.

Darkness changes appearance and privacy. Film construction changes properties such as clarity, color, solar performance and price.

02 Daytime privacy is not nighttime privacy.

Many reflective building films depend on brighter light outside. When the room is brighter at night, the effect can reverse.

03 Film has to match the glass.

Vehicle glass, insulated home windows and commercial panes have different construction, coatings, frames and installation limits.

04 Fresh film needs time to dry.

Installation uses water and slip solution. Temporary moisture haze can remain while the adhesive dries.

One trade, different glass

The finished film should look uneventful.

Clean prep, the correct pattern, controlled squeegee work and deliberate edges are what make that happen.

Identify

Confirm the glass, existing coatings or film, the environment and the customer’s actual goal.

Clean

Remove dust, adhesive and residue before they become visible under the film.

Fit

Cut for the pane. Heat-form compound automotive curves. Plan seams where commercial glass exceeds film width.

Finish

Remove installation solution, control the edges, inspect the work and explain the drying period.

Before you call

What to send for each quote.

Automotive

Send the year, make and model; which windows need film; whether old tint needs removal; and whether the goal is privacy, a subtle look, a darker finish or a factory-glass match.

Residential

Send inside and outside photos, rough glass sizes, the room, the direction the windows face and whether the concern is glare, daytime privacy, fading or temperature.

Commercial

Send the address, glass count, rough measurements, photos, the problem, access limitations and preferred installation timing.
